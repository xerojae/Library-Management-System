FAQ & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long can I borrow a book?
A: All digital and physical resources have a standard borrowing period of 14 days.

Q: How are fines calculated?
A: Fines accrue at a rate of $0.50 per day after the due date. Use the 'Calculate/Update Fine' button to check your balance.

Q: I can't find a book. What do I do?
A: Try searching by the author's name instead of the title. If it is still missing, use the support form below to request it.
